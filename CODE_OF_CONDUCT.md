# Code of Conduct

This is a small hobby project. Everyone is welcome here, regardless of
background, identity or experience.

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks.

If someone makes you uncomfortable, let the maintainer
([@tobiaswild](https://github.com/tobiaswild)) know. Comments, issues or pull
requests that break these rules may be edited or removed, and repeat offenders
may be blocked from the repository.
